Loopweft

A studio for beautiful, animated causal systems maps.

Draw a causal map, cite its evidence, and tell its story step by step: links trace in, pulses follow the signs round a loop, and every claim keeps its source and page. Present it with a presenter console, or share it as one HTML file.

  • Open source under the MIT licence.
  • Local files only: no accounts, no server, no telemetry.
  • A documented command-line tool, so AI agents can build maps for people who want that.
Under construction. The Studio's Map, Sources and Story workspaces are built: draw a map, import one, find its loops, cite its evidence, and tell its story step by step (in Chrome or Edge). Watch the example story in the viewer (use the arrow keys). The presenter and the exports arrive in later phases.
